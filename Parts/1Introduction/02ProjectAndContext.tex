\section{Project and Context}
This work is the pre-project for our Master thesis where the task given to us was as follows:

\blockquote{
    \textbf{[Game technology] Alternative games} \\
    In this project, the goal is to prototype an innovative game(s) and test this game(s) on users. The innovation can be in the type of gameplay the game provides, how it combines various game genres, what technology is used to control or play the game, how the social interaction between players is supported, the purpose of the game, etc.
}

The first phase of the project will include a study of alternative games, game technologies, and concepts, the second phase will be to implement one or more prototypes, and the third phase will be to evaluate the concept through playtesting on real users. The specialization project will mainly focus on part one, while the master thesis period will focus on the second and third phases. 

The project consists of defining alternative games and exploring various concepts in this area. Our work will contribute to the alternative games field of game technology by defining and evaluating a new and promising concept.
