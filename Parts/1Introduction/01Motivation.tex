\section{Motivation}
\label{chap:motivation}
In this project, we are faced with the task of inventing and developing an alternative game concept. Our main motivation for selecting this task was the possibility to be able to develop something that can be socially beneficial. The resulting alternative game should to some degree help an individual or the society as a whole. This does not need to be in a big way, minor improvements matter as well. A To-Do list that assists you to keep track of things, or an application that increases your training motivation, are examples of applications that we define as socially beneficial. Our motivation can also be seen in conjunction with the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic, where we hope the resulting game could be a positive detail in an otherwise boring everyday life. 

An essential aspect of this work that is largely correlated with our motivation, is the availability of the solution. The game needs to be available to as many players as possible. If not, the purpose diminishes to some degree. When comparing and choosing concepts and technologies, this should be kept in mind.

A game genre often associated with socially beneficial applications is gamification. Examples include education-focused (edutainment) and exercise focused (exergames) games. The goal of gamification is to support value creation for the user so that they can get a sense of motivation, achievement, and mastery \cite{hamari_social_2013}. A gamification concept may therefore be an appropriate solution based on our motivation. There exist multiple success stories where gamification concepts have sparked from the idea of helping people to educate themselves and improve their physical and mental well-being. One such example is Strava. This application uses gamification to engage and motivate people to work out and thus can be categorized as a Social Network Game. Users are rewarded with positive reinforcement from social interaction with likes, comments, achieving good results, and breaking records. Social Network Games are proven to correlate with a user’s mental and physical health \citep{pantic_online_2014, mentzoni_problematic_2011}.

In the next few chapters, we will first introduce the context of our task and then define the research goal and questions. After that, we will examine different aspects of creating an alternative game. The motivation presented in this chapter will be essential considerations that will largely influence our forthcoming decisions.
