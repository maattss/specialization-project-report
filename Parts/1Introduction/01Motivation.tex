\section{Motivation}
\label{chap:motivation}
In the era of social networking, people have an insatiable need for constant entertainment. Young adults, in particular, are the first generation growing up with social networking sites (SNS) like Facebook and games (SNG) like World of Warcraft. However, the extent of this is still unclear.

In the later years, there have been conducted multiple studies to find out what consequences modern technology has on young adults. Many of these studies examine the direct effects of SNS and SNG. Studies like the ones performed by \citeauthor{pantic_online_2014} in \citeyear{pantic_online_2014} and \citeauthor{mentzoni_problematic_2011} in \citeyear{mentzoni_problematic_2011}, concluded that SNS and SNG in fact do correlate to users’ mental and physical health. 

Many existing alternative games use gamification and other related concepts to motivate and engage the users in their responsibilities, such as edutainment games, exergames, and well-being games. The goal of gamification is to support value creation for the user so that he or she can get a sense of motivation, achievement, and mastery \citep{hamari_social_2013}.

There exist multiple success stories of alternative games that have sparked from the idea of helping people to educate themselves and improve their physical and mental well-being. One such example is Strava. This application can be seen as both a SNS and SNG because it uses gamification to engage and motivate people to work out. Users are rewarded with positive reinforcement from social interaction with likes, comments, achieving good results, and breaking records.

Based on this, it is a great motivation for us to produce something that can be socially beneficial. We want to come up with a concept that is based on a socially beneficial purpose. This is also motivated by the fact that we would like to create something that we can benefit from ourselves, whether it is social or health benefits.
