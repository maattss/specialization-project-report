\section{Reader's Guide}
This report consists of four main parts. Below is a short description of each of them, together with some reader guidelines. 

\subsection*{Part \ref{part:introduction} - Introduction}
Part \ref{part:introduction} introduces the project, describes the task, and our motivation for choosing it, together with some context around the topic of alternative games. To present our contribution in a structured and understanding way, the research goal and questions that should be answered were defined.

The introduction part is recommended reading for everyone interested in getting an overview of this report’s content and the purpose of this research.

\subsection*{Part \ref{part:prestudy} - Prestudy}
Part \ref{part:prestudy} presents relevant prestudy content. The part includes a literature review of how to make a game enjoyable. It also defines what an alternative game is and describes some already existing concepts. Lastly, It presents a technology review of what is possible with today’s technology in this field. 

This part is particularly useful for all readers that do not have any prior knowledge of the alternative games field. Although the part may be relevant for all, readers familiar with the concept may find this part less relevant.


\subsection*{Part \ref{part:contribution} - Our Contribution}
Part \ref{part:contribution} presents our contribution to the field of alternative games, where we will develop a new alternative game. Chapter \ref{chap:ideas} starts by introducing and evaluating six new alternative game concepts. Chapter \ref{chap:chosen_concept} continues presenting the one concept that will be developed and the reasoning behind the choice. Chapter \ref{chap:game_design} then follows with an extended description of the game and game design. This chapter applies game design theory from chapters \ref{chap:designanenjoyable}-\ref{chap:rewardsystems} to the concept, including relevant game mechanics, player types, and reward mechanisms. Finally, Chapter \ref{chap:chosen_tech} finishes this part by presenting the chosen technology and the selection process.

This part describes the central aspects of the project by presenting our findings and contributions to the field of alternative games. Thus this part is recommended reading for most readers of this report.

\subsection*{Part \ref{part:discussion} - Discussion \& Conclusion}
Part \ref{part:discussion} starts by discussing the choices and challenges encountered during this project in Chapter \ref{chap:discussion}. Chapter \ref{chap:conclusion} concludes the project by answering the defined research questions. Finally, Chapter \ref{chap:further_work} wraps up the specialization project by briefly describing further work planned for the next phase of this project, which also will be our master’s thesis.

Similar to Part \ref{part:contribution}, the discussion and conclusion are recommended reading for most readers. The conclusion is particularly useful for getting a more thorough explanation of the results of our work than the abstract provides. Further work is for the curious readers and provides a glimpse into our plan for the next phases of the project.
