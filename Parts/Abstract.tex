Today’s alternative games are diverse, yet no clear definition of the game category exists in literature. This project aims to investigate how to design alternative game concepts that have a socially beneficial purpose. The research goal is decomposed into four research questions that will be answered: \textit{What makes a game engaging? What is an alternative game? How can alternative games be categorized and conceptualized? Which alternative game concepts can be developed with existing technology that has a socially beneficial purpose?}

The report proceeds to answer these questions through an extensive prestudy consisting of both a literature study and a review of existing alternative games. The report highlights important game design principles for designing enjoyable, motivating, and engaging games. Additionally, research on alternative games and existing games is used to propose a definition for alternative games, along with a model for categorizing and developing new alternative games. This model is used to develop six socially beneficial game concepts, one of which is refined and developed further. 

The chosen concept, \textit{Hover}, is a game that motivates players to do real-world activities. A multitude of game design principles is applied to the concept. Furthermore, suggestions for technology are proposed for future development. 

This project’s work serves as the foundation for our master’s thesis that we will work on during the spring of 2021.
