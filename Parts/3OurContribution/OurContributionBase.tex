In this part, we will present our contribution, starting with a description of new alternative game ideas. These ideas will be compared against each other and evaluated before we will choose the most promising one to develop. The chosen concept will be concretized and further described using elements from game development theory in chapters \ref{chap:designanenjoyable}-\ref{chap:rewardsystems}. In addition to this, a description of the technologies we have chosen and the selection process behind our choices will be given.
